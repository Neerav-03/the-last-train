# THE LAST TRAIN: Story Bible (Director's draft)

*Draft by THE DIRECTOR OF DREAD after two debate rounds with THE LOREKEEPER. It adopts the Lorekeeper's hidden canon as the dev-only truth, with amendments, and governs how that canon reaches the screen. Where this bible and `docs/STORY_REFINEMENT_GUIDE.md` disagree, the guide's §5 invariants win.*

**How to use it.** Part I is sealed: writers, artists and engineers read it, players never get it as text. Parts II-X say what the player actually sees and hears, and when.

**The governing test** applies to every line, object, skin and station: *name the moment it reaches the player and the feeling it produces.* If you can name neither, the fact stays in Part I.

## Part I. The sealed canon (DEV ONLY, never delivered as exposition)

The train is the 2:17 AM Down Local of the **Mandal Circular Railway (M.C.R.)**. It was the last scheduled service on a line that closed decades ago. It left Platform 4 on the line's final night and never arrived. The line was withdrawn and its terminus, HOME, demolished, which is why Home's tracks simply stop. The train is still running the schedule it was never allowed to finish.

### R1. Train time
- Aboard, it is always 2:17. A watch that boards takes on the train's time.
- **Amendment:** the Businessman's watch is the one object still fighting this. It holds the real times the train is erasing: his 6:40 and, across replays, the candidate's (see Part VI).
- No minute has ever passed aboard except the one minute of 2:18 the brake buys (R5).

### R2. Who the train stops for
- It stops for someone alone on a platform at 2:17 whose night is unfinished, and who in that minute cannot bear to arrive where they're going.
- Nobody remembers boarding. You don't choose to board; you fail to refuse.

### R3. The seven calls
- **Calls 1-4 are borrowed.** Each is a platform lifted from one rider's unfinished night:
  - Kalyanpur: the Businessman
  - Madhav Nagar: the Woman
  - Sector 0: the Rememberer, together with the Conductor
  - [UNLISTED]: the candidate. It is your hometown station, which is why it's unlisted: you aren't on the manifest.
- **Calls 5-7 belong to the train:**
  - The Empty Platform is where confiscated memories are left.
  - Home is the terminus.
  - The Last Stop is ticket inspection, held in the carriage.
- This split is the in-fiction reason for Invariant 2.

### R4. Tickets and the Conductor
- Every rider holds a ticket.
- The Conductor (Guard D. Pillai, M.C.R.) collects only at the Last Stop, and only from the candidate. The open palm is a ticket inspection.
- He is a bureaucrat, not a villain. He is still on duty and can't finish a run whose terminus is gone.

### R5. Three exits
- **getOff (set down short).** You give up your ticket. The train keeps the memory of the ride as your fare and leaves it on the Empty Platform. Your unfinished thing is untouched, so some future night you'll be on Platform 4 again, as yourself.
- **stayOn.** You keep your ticket and your name goes on the manifest. You become a rider, and the next night the candidate is someone new.
- **breakLoop.** The emergency brake is crew equipment that sits outside the train's clock. Pulling it gives the carriage one minute of 2:18.
  - Memory aboard must always be carried by exactly one person, the **Rememberer**.
  - Whoever pulls the brake keeps their memory and becomes the new Rememberer. The train takes the Rememberer's face so nobody aboard can be reminded by it.
  - The previous Rememberer is released into 2:18.
  - Breaking the loop ends your cycle by making you the part of the machine that remembers. It is a trade, not a victory.

### R6. Witnessing
- A rider's illusion thins when someone *not on the manifest* sees it clearly.
- The candidate is always unlisted, so only the candidate can witness. The child fades from trip 3 because of your attention.
- **Amendment (Director):** reflections in the carriage glass show the riders' *unwitnessed* version of the world. The glass is the illusion and the room is the truth. This rule drives set-piece B.

### R7. True arrival
- True arrival means handing in your ticket *and* walking through Home's door.
- It has happened exactly once, decades ago: S. Rao, the Old Man's wife.
- The long-term expansion goal is clearing the manifest. It is never stated as a goal on screen.

### R8. Nightly reset
- The same night repeats. Riders reset when they step off at the Last Stop (the Procession, set-piece F).
- Only the manifest and the Rememberer accumulate. That is exactly what the meta-save stores.

### R9. The player is not new (Director addition, made explicit)
- Every getOff wiped your memory of the ride. Kalyanpur's "every other night you've taken this train" is literally true.
- This is why the Silent Passenger says "You weren't on the list", why the bench initials exist, and why "You weren't supposed to remember" is the line it is.

### R10. The Empty Platform figure (Director addition)
- The figure is a confiscated memory: the left-behind fare of a rider who was set down short.
- On a first run it is a stranger's. After you choose getOff, it is yours (Part VI).
- It speaks only one line, "You shouldn't have gotten off", which is a memory warning its owner.

## Part II. Never shown on screen

These are absolute. Each one protects a feeling the game cannot buy back once it's spent.

**Identity**
1. **The Silent Passenger's face.**
   - It is always in shadow, at every distance and under every light.
   - In 3D, the head sits inside a light-null volume, and no light, reflection probe or flash reaches it.
   - It is never shown in the same frame as the player's own reflection, because that would confirm the "it's you" reading.
2. **Any statement of who the Silent Passenger is.**
   - They never use "I" about the past, never explain the Rememberer role, and are never shown receiving or handing over memory.
   - Four readings must stay live: (a) it's you, (b) it's the train's agent, (c) it's the first passenger, (d) it's death.
3. **The player's real name.**
   - The game never asks for it and never renders one. "Your name" stays a description.
   - Skin names (Part VI) are not the player's name. One may be heard on the replay phone (set-piece G) precisely because it is *not yours anymore*.

**Events and places**
4. **What happened on the original night.** The newspaper stays torn mid-sentence forever. "Fails to reach terminus" is the most anyone ever says.
5. **Whether anyone aboard is dead.** No grave, no body, no obituary, and no "you died".
6. **Where the child went.**
7. **What lies beyond Home's door.** It opens a crack, never fully (set-piece E).
8. **What getOff leads to.** The street "looks almost right" and nothing more.
9. **What happens after breakLoop.** The final card stays empty of text (see Part IX, item 2).

**Rules and mechanics**
10. **The rules of the loop as rules.** No codex, no tutorial voice, no loop counter, no "Night N" UI.
11. **The M.C.R. full name.** On screen it is only ever the three letters, on a poster and on screws.
12. **Any rider's full name in speech.** Names exist on paper only, and only on replay (Part IX).
13. **The STATUS word on a first run.** It renders only when the loop remembers you (run 2+).

**Characters**
14. **The Conductor's name, badge or past.**
15. **Flashbacks, cutaways, diaries, monologues and backstory dumps.** No passenger ever narrates their wound in more than one line.

## Part III. Passenger bible

Each passenger has five parts:
- **Wound:** sealed.
- **Behavior:** shown.
- **Beats:** authored lines and objects.
- **Never said:** an absolute ban.
- **Exhausted state:** what happens after their final stage, so they never loop their reveal line.

### The Old Man (Govind Rao, "the first rider")

**Wound.** He and his wife Sarla held two tickets for the original 2:17. He missed it and she boarded. Years later he boarded to find her. She is the only person who ever truly arrived, and he rides the train she has already left. "Don't miss your stop" is his guilt, said out loud. He is the one who scratched the station names off the maps, over decades.

**Behavior**
- His head turns a few degrees further toward the candidate every trip. By trip 6 he faces you fully, which pays off "eyes the color of the window glass".
- If you pressed him (`oldManPressed`), he stops volunteering and waits for you to speak first. If you stayed silent (`oldManIgnored`), he talks to the window instead.
- His hands drift from the cane into the Silent Passenger's exact folded-hands pose by trip 6. This makes the Stay On line "hands folded the way the old man folds his" quietly true and quietly wrong.
- He never looks at the window seat beside him.

**Beats**
- Existing stages are kept.
- New, minTrip 5: "She had the window seat. I had the tickets." It is one line and never elaborated.
- `oldTicketStub` card gains: "On the back: ADULT ×2. It was never punched."
- Home clock: the tally scratched around the rim (Part IX). He made it, and nobody ever says so.
- Last Stop, if `oldManRevealed`: the Conductor's palm hesitates at your pocket. "That one's not yours to give."

**Never said**
- Sarla's name.
- That she arrived.
- That he scratched the maps.
- How long he has ridden.

**Exhausted state.** He turns back to the window and says nothing. The dialogue box shows his action only: "[He's looking at the empty seat beside him. Then he isn't.]"

### The Woman (Meera)

**Wound.** Her son died at the hospital near Madhav Nagar. She couldn't go home to his room, and sat on that station's bench until 2:17. The train keeps her in the minute before she was told. The child is the memory she carries, and it fades from trip 3 because you witness it (R6).

**Behavior**
- She rocks in time with the wheel rhythm and stops when the train stops.
- After `childGone` she keeps rocking at stations, out of rhythm with nothing.
- If `comfortedWoman`, she slides over a seat and leaves the space beside her empty for you on every later trip.
- If `avoidedWoman`, she turns her back to the aisle.
- Before `childGone`, the child's head occasionally turns to face you. It happens only in the window reflection, never in the room (set-piece B).

**Beats**
- Madhav Nagar's one wrong detail (Part V) is an infant-sized hospital wristband under the warm spot on the bench, with no name on it. It is her station, and the payoff lands one trip later at `childGone`.
- Post-`comfortedWoman`: "They wrote the time down for me. I left it on a card somewhere." This retroactively explains Madhav Nagar's blank index card. Nobody points it out.
- Procession (set-piece F): she carries her arms as if the child is still in them, stops at your seat, and holds the empty air out to you (Tier 1: Take it / Don't).
- Last Stop, inspecting `emptySeats` near her seat: her ticket, stamped "PLATFORM ADMISSION. NOT VALID FOR TRAVEL."

**Never said**
- The child's name.
- "Died", "hospital" or "dead".
- The child's age.

**Exhausted state.** She is only rocking. "[She doesn't look up.]" This is her first line returning, so the end rhymes with the start.

### The Businessman (Vikram Sethi)

**Wound.** He stayed for a meeting and missed the 6:40 to his dying father. He got off at the hospital's station at 2:17 and couldn't walk the last block. While the watch reads 2:17 he is only running late, never too late. When he vanishes, he has stepped off to try to arrive, and failed with no one to witness it.

**Behavior**
- He checks his watch whenever the player opens the clue journal: he mirrors you.
- **Not the vanisher:** from trip 5 he is standing at the door every time you enter, as if the next stop is his. He never gets off. Once, as you pass: "Is this mine?"
- **The vanisher:** his briefcase stays on the disturbed seat through the Last Stop, where it is the only object the Procession leaves behind.

**Beats**
- Existing arc is kept.
- `businessmanWatch` card gains a caseback engraving: "So you're never late. Papa."
- New line, at the stage after "For you" (the non-vanisher only gets there): "At 2:17 I'm late. At 2:18 I'm... something else." This plants 2:18 for the breakLoop ending.
- Kalyanpur's vending machine (his station) receipt gains one faded line under the existing text: "Below it, an older print, almost gone: 6:38 PM."

**Never said**
- "Father".
- "Died".
- The meeting.

**Exhausted state.** He no longer speaks to you. Interacting shows the watch face, turned toward you, wordless: "[He holds the watch out so you can see it.]"

### The Student (Tanvi)

**Wound.** At 11:52 PM her mother left a voicemail. Her headphones were in and she didn't pick up. Her mother died that night, and Tanvi has never played the message. The static is what she plays instead. She hears *your* name in it because someone on the platform phone is calling you.

**Behavior**
- Her foot taps with the wheels. After trip 3 it drifts off-beat, and by trip 6 it taps in time with the player's own inputs.
- At stage 3 she holds a headphone out. If you don't take it, **her arm stays extended on every later visit** until you do.
- When she finally hands the headphones over, all carriage audio drops out for 2 seconds.
- **If she's the vanisher:** the headphones stay on her seat, hissing, getting louder as you approach. Her phone lies beside them: "1 VOICEMAIL. MAA. 11:52 PM." (Unplayed. The player cannot play it. Never.)

**Beats**
- Existing arc is kept.
- At her final stage, the jack: "The cable ends in nothing. It was never plugged in."
- New, minTrip 5: "It's not my name. I've listened for mine. Mine's under yours."

**Never said**
- What the voicemail says.
- "Mother died".

**Exhausted state.** Headphones are back on, eyes closed, and the foot taps in time with you.

### The Silent Passenger (the current Rememberer)

**Wound (sealed).**
- They are the last person who pulled the brake.
- On a first run they are a stranger. After a breakLoop run, they wear your previous skin.
- They walk the stations alongside each candidate. The Sector 0 footsteps are theirs.

**Behavior**
- They always face the end of the carriage you last came in from, and are never seen turning.
- They use the same footstep sample and cadence as Sector 0 when they cross the aisle at the Last Stop. That audio rhyme is the only way the link is ever made.
- After a breakLoop run they face *you* from trip 1, wearing the coat silhouette of the skin you just left. This is shown, never stated.

**Beats**
- **Change:** the §6.6 vanish-acknowledgement payoff ("You looked away. Most do." / "You held it. That's rarer.") moves to the **Old Man**. The Silent Passenger's version becomes non-verbal: "[Their head tilts, almost imperceptibly, toward the empty seat.]"
- "You weren't on the list" stays their **true first words**. Make it reachable: `minTrip: 6`, `minInteractions: 2`.
- "You weren't supposed to remember" stays their second and last line.
- **Procession:** if the player never interacted with them all run, they are the one who *doesn't* rise.

**Never said**
- Anything past their two lines.
- No "I", no "we", no "again".

**Exhausted state.** "[They do not move.]" This is permanent.

### The Conductor (Guard D. Pillai, M.C.R.)

**Wound.** He is the guard of the original service, still on duty.

**Behavior**
- He is glimpsed exactly once before the finale: on trip 4, through the gangway-door window at the far end of the carriage, walking away. He appears for about 3s and only if the camera or player is facing that end.
- At the Last Stop, "You have never seen him before" becomes a line the attentive player *knows is false*. Keep it.

**Never said**
- His name.
- What happens to the tickets.
- "Fare".

## Part IV. Set-pieces and the jumpscare budget

**Jumpscare budget**
- **Two on a first run**: D and F.
- **One held back for replays**: G2.
- No other beat may use a stinger plus a sudden reveal together.

**Every set-piece follows these rules**
- It checks `settings.reduceFlashing` and `settings.screenShake`, with an authored quieter fallback (Invariant 9).
- It never blocks progression (Invariant 8).
- It touches no station-pool clue (Invariants 5 and 6).

| ID | Name | Where | Canon hook | Jumpscare | DOM-feasible now? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Tunnel Sweep | Trip 7 travel phase | R5/R9: the Rememberer closes in before inspection | No (anti-scare) | Partial; full in 3D |
| B | Reflections | Every tunnel phase from trip 2 | R6: the glass shows the unwitnessed version | No | No, 3D only |
| C | Half a Beat Behind | Sector 0 | Rememberer's footsteps | No | Yes |
| D | The Silent Return | Empty Platform reveal | R3/R10: the train won't set you down here yet | **#1** | Yes |
| E | Dinner's Almost Ready | Home, after the reveal | R3/R7: the demolished terminus replays memory | No | Yes (text plus procedural murmur) |
| F | The Procession | Last Stop | R8: riders reset as they step off | **#2** (audio, in blackout) | Yes |
| G | The Call | Platform, run 2+ | Student wound: someone is calling you | G2 on run 3+ = **#3** | Yes |

### A. Tunnel Sweep (trip 7, the post-Home ride)
This fixes the worst sag in the current build.
- **Setup:** during the trip 7 travel phase, the carriage bulbs die in sequence from the far end toward the player, extending `deadLightIds` to all five. The only light left is tunnel lamps sweeping through the windows every ~4s, real moving light across faces.
- **The changes:** between sweeps, one passenger's pose or seat shifts, picked by `rngFor(runSeed,'sweep:'+i)`. The Silent Passenger is one row closer each sweep.
- **The payoff:** the door unlocks only after the last sweep. The player walks to it in the dark. As they reach it, the bulbs snap back on, **everyone is exactly where they started**, and the Silent Passenger is in their seat, facing forward. No release, no scare. The tension carries into the Last Stop.
- **DOM version:** `FlickerLight` pools sweep left to right, and passenger slots swap pose classes while the pool is off them.
- **Accessibility:** with `reduceFlashing`, sweeps become 1.5s soft gradients instead of hard-edged pulses.

### B. Reflections (3D, every tunnel phase from trip 2)
- **When:** whenever the exterior is tunnel-black, the windows render as mirrors. The reflection is the *riders' version* of the carriage (R6):
  - **Trip 2+:** the `vanishingPassengerId` passenger is still seated in the glass.
  - **Before `childGone`:** the child's head turns toward you in the glass only.
  - **After `childGone`:** the Woman's reflection still holds the child.
  - **Trip 5+:** your reflection is seated in seat 6 while you stand.
- **Rules:**
  - Never call attention to it: no sound cue, no prompt.
  - It shows only when the camera happens to face the glass during a tunnel.
  - The Silent Passenger has *no* reflection, ever. Their seat in the glass is simply empty. This is the strongest single "is it them, or me?" image the game has, and it confirms nothing.

### C. Half a Beat Behind (Sector 0)
- **What happens:** from the player's first inspect, each inspect is echoed by a `playFootstep()` ~400ms later.
  - In 3D it is positional, directly behind the camera, and it stops when the camera turns toward it.
  - When the player goes idle, it takes *one more step* than they did.
  - Then the existing `followFootsteps` hotspot reveals itself. Replace the flat 13s timer with "after the 2nd echoed inspect, or 13s, whichever is first", so nobody boards before it exists.
- **Existing systems:** the §6.3 threshold, clue (`unseenCompanion`) and invariants are unchanged.
- **Audio rhyme:** use the same footstep sample and cadence for the Silent Passenger at the Last Stop (F).

### D. The Silent Return (Empty Platform, JUMPSCARE #1)
- **The figure:** it moves only while unobserved. In the DOM, it advances one position step whenever a dialogue box covers it. In 3D, it moves while off-camera. Its existing inspect text ("It is closer now. You didn't see it move.") is kept, and is now *true*.
- **The reveal:** the train returns **without sound**. The ambience is already `silence`, so cut even the rail hum. The stinger fires only when the player turns, or the DOM camera pans, back toward the tracks and the lit open doorway is beside them.
  - The doorway is empty. Never put a figure in it, so every reading stays live.
  - Then "You shouldn't have gotten off." comes from the figure's direction, though the figure is gone.
- **Keep:** fixed position and timing (Invariant 2), and `EMPTY_PLATFORM_REVEAL_FALLBACK_MS`.
- **`reduceFlashing`:** no light change. The stinger plays at -6 dB, and the doorway fades in over 600ms instead of cutting.

### E. Dinner's Almost Ready (Home, after the void reveal)
- **Setup:** Home's fixed reveal (void plus `RedEmergencyOverlay`) is untouched. After it, the Door Home is the only warm thing left, and it becomes openable once.
- **What happens:**
  - It opens a crack. Warm light, a radio murmur, pans.
  - A voice from inside: "Dinner's almost ready."
  - Then the **identical** line again: same text, same box, same timing, same murmur sample.
  - Then a third time.
- **The ending:** the door closes on its own. The player has worked out for themselves that the warmth is a recording.
- **Assets:** use the PA announcement line already in `stations.ts`, rendered in a "close, human" dialogue style instead of PA SYSTEM. The murmur is procedural (filtered noise plus formant hum, no voice asset). It does not count toward progression.

### F. The Procession (Last Stop, JUMPSCARE #2)
The text-box line "One by one, the remaining passengers rise" becomes staged.

**1. The walk-out.** Each passenger stands, walks the aisle past your seat toward the door, and does one gesture:

| Passenger | Gesture |
|---|---|
| Old Man | Touches the brim of a cap he isn't wearing |
| Woman | Holds the empty air out to you (Tier 1 choice) |
| Businessman | Taps his watch at you. If he vanished, his briefcase stays behind on the seat |
| Student | If you never took the headphone, she sets it on your seat as she passes |
| The vanished passenger | Walks out with the others, back for this one moment only |

**2. The approach.** The Silent Passenger stands and crosses the aisle with the Sector 0 footstep cadence. Or, if the player never once interacted with them, they don't rise, and the scene skips straight to the blackout.

**3. The blackout.** The existing 1.5s blackout, extended to 2.5s. In total darkness, **one slow breath at the player's ear**, using the platform payphone's breathing.

**4. Lights return** on the Conductor (existing `INTRO_PART_2`). The palm hesitates on the Old Man's stub if `oldManRevealed`.

**Notes**
- Audio-only in darkness, so it is safe under `reduceFlashing` with no fallback needed.
- `breakLoop` must no longer repeat the "silent passenger finally stands" lines (Part IX).

### G. The Call (Platform, replay only)
- **Run 2+:** the payphone is *ringing* when the scene loads. Answering it plays breathing, then a flat voice reads the last line of your previous ending ("You just don't remember leaving." / "There are six passengers aboard." / *silence for breakLoop: the line is just breathing, longer*).
- **Run 3+ (`nightsRidden >= 3`):** the voice says one name, the **display name of the skin you wore last night**, then hangs up. Not the player's name, not the current skin. The caption reads: *"…[name]?"*
- **G2, Run 3+, JUMPSCARE #3 (once per meta-save, seeded 1-in-3 per eligible run):**
  - **Where:** trip 1 carriage, the room the player has learned is safe.
  - **Trigger:** the first time a dialogue box closes during trip 1.
  - **The scare:** the most recent stayOn skin is standing in the aisle at arm's length. If there is none, use the Silent Passenger, face in shadow. Stinger.
  - **Then:** on the next input they are seated, head down, as if nothing happened.
  - **Rule:** it never fires again in that save (`meta.reserveScareSpent`).

### 1. Travel phase
- **The problem:** the carriage currently plays `trainStopped` throughout, and the door teleports you to the next station. The train never moves while you're on it.
- **The fix:** every carriage visit opens **in motion**:
  - `setAmbience('trainMoving')`, wheel rhythm, tunnel or exterior darkness at the windows.
  - Duration comes from `rngFor(runSeed,'travel:'+tripNumber)`, within these bands: trip 1 = 50-60s, trips 2-5 = 40-55s, trips 6-7 = 70-90s.
  - It ends with brakes, `trainStopped`, then the door unlocks.
- **This is where change happens.** Micro-drift (dead lights, seat drift, route map) applies at tunnel-dark moments *during* the ride, never between scene loads. The player should catch it changing.
- **Run length:** this alone takes the run from roughly 8-15 to roughly 20-25 minutes.

### 2. Door lock
- The door is sealed (the existing `carriage-door--sealed` class) during the travel phase.
- No "locked" text. It just doesn't respond, plus a dull thunk.
- **Hard ceiling:** the door always unlocks at the band maximum (Invariant 8 spirit).

### 3. minTrip gating and the per-visit cap
- **Cap:** a passenger gives at most 2 *new* stages per carriage visit.
- **Exception:** the run's `vanishingPassengerId`, which is uncapped on trip 1 so their arc is never orphaned (Invariant 1 rationale).
- **Final-reveal gates** (passenger-awarded clues are outside the `requiredClueIds` pool, so this is invariant-safe):

  | Passenger | Final stage gate |
  |---|---|
  | Businessman "Seven… For you" | `minTrip: 4`, ignored if he is the vanisher |
  | Student name-in-static | `minTrip: 4`, ignored if she is the vanisher |
  | Old Man "waiting longer than you" | `minTrip: 5` (never vanishes) |
  | Silent Passenger "You weren't on the list" | `minTrip: 6`, `minInteractions: 2` |

- **Implementation:** a new optional `minTripUnlessVanisher` field in `DialogueStage`, resolved in `TrainInterior.tsx` `resolveStage()`.

### 4. Exhausted passengers go non-verbal
- After a passenger's final stage, further interaction shows their **exhausted state** action line (Part III).
- It never replays the reveal. The clue is already awarded, and `awardClue` is idempotent.

### 5. The vanish, nearly witnessed
- On trip 2, the vanisher is present when the travel phase begins.
- At the first tunnel-dark moment (a ~1.2s light dip), the seat empties. When the light returns, it is disturbed and empty.
- The player never sees them go, only the moment they missed. Seeded timing.

### 6. The 2:17 budget
Today it appears about 10 times, and every repeat weakens it.

**Rule: four in-scene appearances plus one ending payoff.** Clue cards may reference it but count against the same budget, so prefer cutting there.

| Keep | Change |
|---|---|
| Platform clock (the opening) | Home clock: no number (Part IX) |
| Businessman's watch arc (counts as one, including his 2:18 line) | Ticket clue keeps it (it's the player's own ticket) |
| [Unlisted] timetable "ARRIVAL 2:17 AM / DEPARTURE NEVER" | `oldTicketStub` gets "ADULT ×2" instead of the time |
| Last Stop sign "2:17 AM" | `conductorsLedger` keeps it (final card) |
| Stay On ending "It reads 2:17" (payoff) | Kalyanpur poster and newspaper never show it |

- **Ending payoffs:** each ending gets at most one 2:17. Stay On has its line. breakLoop has the silent corner clock that ticks to 2:18 (Part IX #2). Get Off has none.
- **"Your name" budget:** three appearances (billboard, static recording, ledger). Do not add more.

### 7. Madhav Nagar becomes the false calm
- No dread ambience.
- Lights steady: the one station where `FlickerLight` doesn't flicker.

## Part VI. Reincarnation and meta-save: the merged design

**The goal, as experience:** a returning player should feel that the loop noticed them. They should suspect themselves first ("did it always say that?") and the game second, and should never be told.

### Data model (adopting the Lorekeeper's model, extended)

Persist under a separate localStorage key **`lastTrain.meta.v1`**.
- `startNewGame()` never clears it. A new **Settings → "Let the line forget you"** option does, with diegetic copy and a confirmation step. This is also the QA reset.
- It is written once in `setEnding()` and read once in `startNewGame()`.

```ts
// src/engine/meta.ts (new)
export interface ManifestEntry {
  nightNumber: number;
  runSeed: number;
  skinId: string;
  initials: string;
  ending: EndingKey;
  seatAffinity: string | null;     // max-interaction passenger id (same logic as Endings.tsx seatLoyaltyLine)
  keptItem: string | null;         // e.g. 'oldTicketStub', 'receipt', 'headphone'
  comfortedWoman: boolean;
  heldGaze: boolean;
  endedAtLocalTime: string;        // "3:42" — real wall-clock time setEnding fired (Director addition)
  inspectEcho: string[];           // first 6 inspect line texts the player saw this run (Director addition, for the sixth-passenger echo)
}
export interface MetaSave {
  version: 1;
  nightsRidden: number;            // completed runs
  currentSkinId: string;
  manifest: ManifestEntry[];       // append-only, capped at 12 (oldest dropped)
  rememberer: { skinId: string; sinceNight: number } | null;
  benchInitials: string[];         // capped at 6
  reserveScareSpent: boolean;      // set-piece G2
  lastForgotNight: number | null;  // anti-gimmick "forgetting run" bookkeeping
}
// src/data/skins.ts (new)
export interface SkinDef {
  id: string; displayName: string; initials: string;
  hometownName: string;            // [UNLISTED] wrong-shop sign text on runs in this skin
  silhouetteId: string;            // coat/posture variant for Platform.tsx player + 3D rig
  carriedItem: string;             // pocket object, flavor only
  unfinishedLine: string;          // ONE line, used only by the platform phone (G) and never elsewhere
}
```

Skin pick happens in `startNewGame()`:
- Use `pick(rngFor(runSeed,'skin'), SKINS.filter(s => !inManifest(s)))` when the last ending changes skin.
- Otherwise keep `currentSkinId`.
- If the pool is exhausted, reuse the oldest dropped skin.

**Hard rules (binding, extending the guide's §5)**
- **M1.** Meta drives only leaf, cosmetic and Tier 1 text, plus optional bonus content. It never touches `requiredClueIds`, the vanish pool, any station-pool clue's availability, the structure of stations 5-7, or any fallback timer.
- **M2.** A run with an empty meta-save (`nightsRidden === 0`) plays exactly as the canonical first run. All replay content is additive and gated on `nightsRidden >= 1`.
- **M3.** The sixth-passenger entities (stayOn skins) are never in the vanish pool, never interactable for clues, and never counted in `seatLoyaltyLine`.

### Per-ending carryover (resolving the getOff conflict)

**The conflict.** The Lorekeeper's getOff kept the "same skin", while my getOff showed "a figure in your coat walking off the platform".

**The resolution: both, and they mean the same thing.** With getOff you remain yourself (same skin, R5: your unfinished thing is untouched). What you see leaving up the stairs is your *memory of the ride*, the fare the train kept. The same silhouette is the Empty Platform figure that night (R10).

| Last ending | Next night's skin | Run 2 (deniable) | Run 3+ (bold) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **getOff** | Same skin | Arrival: a figure in *your* coat walks up the platform stairs as you board. Bench: one more set of initials, fresher. Empty Platform edge: "One coin, right at the edge. Exactly where you'd throw it." | The Empty Platform figure has your silhouette. Ledger card gains a row in the SET DOWN SHORT column with your initials. Coins: "A small pile." |
| **stayOn** | New skin | **The sixth passenger:** your old skin sits beside your last run's `seatAffinity` passenger, head down, never speaking. Interacting shows your own `inspectEcho` lines from last night, one per interaction, in order. The route map has one extra unlabeled dot from trip 1. | Older stayOn skins appear through the gangway-door window in the next carriage: more silhouettes each time, never countable. Their initials are added to the Passenger List (WAITING itself is universal from run 2, Part VIII #3). |
| **breakLoop** | New skin | The Silent Passenger faces *you* from trip 1, in your previous skin's coat silhouette. The Call is breathing only. | Passenger List: the previous Rememberer's row is struck through. No word, never "ARRIVED". |

**Universal replay beats** (any prior ending, drawn from the per-run budget below)
- Old Man, first line: "Don't miss your stop." … "Again." (deniable)
- The Call (G).
- Businessman's watch, when held as a clue card on run 3+: it reads `manifest[last].endedAtLocalTime`. This is the one place 2:17 ever breaks, and it breaks toward the player's real life (R1 amendment).
- Old Man, minTrip 5 on run 3+: "You're starting to look like you've been here a while." Never a count. He has ridden longer than anyone, and the line must not contradict that.
- Student, run 3+: she offers the headphone *before* you've spoken to her.

### Guardrails against the gimmick

1. **Never announce.** No loop counter, no toast, no achievement, no "Night N" UI, no changed title screen text.
2. **Budget per run:** at most **3** memory beats, drawn via `rngFor(runSeed,'memory')` from the eligible pool. Run 2 draws only from the *deniable* tier. Run 3+ may include *bold* beats, at most 1 per run.
3. **Deniability first.** Every run-2 beat must sit in a place the player remembers clearly (first lines, the bench, the arrival) and be small enough that they doubt their memory.
4. **Behavior, not stats.** The loop remembers what you *did* (who you sat with, what you kept, how the night ended), never scores or completion.
5. **No mechanical reward.** Meta never unlocks endings, choices or clues the first run lacked. Bonus text only (M1).
6. **The loop sometimes forgets.** From `nightsRidden >= 4`, there is a 1-in-5 seeded chance of zero memory beats: a run identical to the first. Never two forgetting runs in a row (`lastForgotNight`).
7. **The first run is sacred** (M2).
8. **One reserved scare per save** (G2).

## Part VII. Expansion framework

New content must pass both the canon test (Part I) and the feeling test (governing test). Each new station ships with a **Station Card**:

```ts
// additive fields on StationDef (src/engine/types.ts)
owner: string | 'candidate' | 'train';   // rider id that "owns" this borrowed platform
ownerMoment?: string;                     // DEV ONLY — the unfinished minute this platform is lifted from
era?: 'mcr' | 'post-mcr';                 // post-mcr stations are missing from old maps (incompleteStationMap logic)
feeling: {                                // Director addition — required, reviewed before content is written
  targetDread: number;                    // 1-10, must fit the curve slot it replaces
  oneBeat: string;                        // the single moment the station exists for
  oneWrongness: string;                   // exactly one clock / map / sign wrongness
};
```

### Station rules
1. Only owner-mapped *borrowed* stations enter the mild and moderate bands. Calls 5-7 never change (Invariant 2).
2. **One owner object per station.** It relates to its owner's wound obliquely and is never captioned with it. Existing examples: Kalyanpur's receipt (Businessman), Madhav Nagar's wristband (Woman).
3. **One wrongness per station**, plus the owner object. No more.
4. **No station tells its owner's story in words.** The owner object's text is at most 2 sentences.
5. A new station replaces or variants an existing slot in its band (anomaly-pool style). Never lengthen the run past 30 minutes.

### Passenger template (all seven fields required, all sealed except the behavior)
1. The platform they stood on alone at 2:17.
2. That they have no memory of boarding.
3. The real time the train is erasing.
4. The ticket they won't hand in.
5. What being witnessed would release.
6. Their owned station.
7. The trace they leave if they step off.

Plus the Director fields: **behavior** (at least 3 non-verbal changes across trips), **exhausted state**, **never said**, and **one-line budget** (at most one line that brushes their wound).

### Pipeline examples (unscheduled)
- **Stations:**
  - RADIO COLONY (Student; a tower light blinking the rhythm of her foot tap)
  - PLATFORM 4 (DOWN) (Old Man, decades younger; the bench without initials)
  - CANTONMENT HALT (the Night Nurse)
- **The Night Nurse** (new passenger, outside the vanish pool):
  - She enters via the next carriage, only after Wave G.
  - She left a colleague to tell the Woman, and never looks at the Woman's seat.
  - Bonus line if `comfortedWoman`: "You did the thing I couldn't."
  - Adding her requires a full compressed-arc pass before any vanish-pool discussion (Invariant 1).

### 3D rules (Wave E/H)
- The Silent Passenger head light-null volume.
- No Silent Passenger reflection.
- The face-in-shadow rule survives any camera.
- Darkness is a resource: never more than 3 fully lit carriage visits in a row after trip 3.
